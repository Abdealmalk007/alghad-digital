Kitchen CAD Pro V5 — CPU3D Beta Preview

Extract the entire ZIP and run Launch_KitchenCAD_DESIGN_FEEDBACK_R3_QA.cmd. Use the Panels menu to show/hide the catalog and inspector, F9 to focus the drawing, and the Advanced section to edit catalog templates. This is an unsigned test build with a static CPU 3D preview, not a production-certified program. Do not use CNC, cut lists or BOM for manufacturing. Back up your projects.

Archive size: 94,639,699 bytes. SHA-256 checksums: f6d22ee210893e6cb2250ce22454ef8ece54ab9d8ddbb0f9333eb154a9177500.

New R3 design feedback: precise doors and windows, adjacent cabinets, dimension editing, per-cabinet handle direction, internal accessory previews and editable straight stone/sink/hob cutouts. CPU 3D is static; L/U stone extension and CNC remain unverified.
