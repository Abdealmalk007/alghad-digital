PROTECH / PRACTICAL GUIDE

Prepare employee records and review payroll

  1. Begin with sample records and a unique employee ID; do not use real identity data for testing.
  2. Reconcile attendance, leave and incomplete time records with the correct payroll period and your organization's approved rules.
  3. Manually verify a sample before approving results, retain a protected backup, and restrict access to employee data.
